## 🟢 Evidência consolidada (RCTs / meta-análises)

**Respiração / breathwork**
- Fincham, G. W. et al. (2023). *Effect of breathwork on stress and mental health: A meta-analysis of randomised-controlled trials.* Scientific Reports (Nature).
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-27247-y
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36624160/
- Balban, M. Y. et al. (2023). *Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal.* Cell Reports Medicine.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9873947/
- RCT de *Conscious Connected Breathwork* para ansiedade (2025), Journal of Affective Disorders.
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0165032725020075
- Global Wellness Institute — síntese de evidência em breathwork.
  https://globalwellnessinstitute.org/wellnessevidence/breathwork/

**Núcleo científico do "imprint" (programação fetal e apego)**
- Campo DOHaD (Developmental Origins of Health and Disease) — estresse pré-natal e reatividade ao estresse.
- Teoria do apego (Bowlby) — padrões precoces de vínculo moldam relações adultas.
