## Validation Invariants (from RESEARCH.md §Validation Architecture)

### Invariant 1 — Pre-existing test regression gate
**What:** 266+ existing tests in `test_features/`, `test_sectoral_pigments/`, `test_segment/` stay GREEN.
**Evidence:** `cd vision-service && pytest -q` exits 0 with ≥266 collected/passed.
**Sampling:** Every wave completion + before `/gsd-verify-work`.

### Invariant 2 — Synthetic fixture geometric convergence
**What:** New synthetic close-up fixture (TDD path) — when run through `find_iris → iris_mask`, produces detection dict with `_detector="hough_color_masked"` AND segment circle whose center matches ground-truth iris center within ≤50px.
**Evidence:** `pytest tests/test_detect.py::test_synthetic_closeup_color_mask_recovers_iris` exits 0.
**Sampling:** Every task touching `_color_iris_mask` or `_hough_circle_fallback`.

### Invariant 3 — Empirical fixture geometric convergence (P1 gate)
**What:** Local probe on Nailli + 2-3 D-05 synthetic fixtures (other readings with status='ready', client_id != Nailli, 6 images) shows segment centers ≤50px apart AND radii within ±15% across 3 angles per eye.
**Evidence:** `python scripts/probe_pigment_deltas.py --fixture <id> --dump-detect-table` emits CSV that the planner audits; convergence table is P1 PR evidence.
**Sampling:** Each P1 iteration; convergence trajectory must improve monotonically (stop-loss criterion).

### Invariant 4 — Modal reprocess geometric gate (P2 gate)
**What:** After Modal redeploy + Nailli reprocess, `vision_features.processing_metadata.detect_diagnostics` for reading `71a7bf1d-747f-4de8-9129-13b69197c6a4` shows ≤50px center spread + ≤15% radius CV per eye.
**Evidence:** Inspect Modal response payload OR query Supabase `readings` row.
**Sampling:** Once at end of P2 (after Modal redeploy + reprocess trigger).

### Invariant 5 — Visual gate
**What:** `enhanced_polar` PNG for Nailli post-fix is visually recognizable as iris (radial striated texture filling ≥80% of polar width; no large dark zones).
**Evidence:** Side-by-side comparison `vision-service/scripts/output/71a7bf1d_*_enhanced_polar.png` (pre-fix from baseline commit `8494a4a`) vs post-fix.
**Sampling:** Once at end of P1 + once at end of P2. Founder qualitative gate.

### Invariant 6 — MediaPipe success path non-regression
**What:** Any photo where MediaPipe returns landmarks continues through the existing path; color mask does NOT degrade detection dict.
**Evidence:** `pytest tests/test_detect.py -k face_context` — synthetic fixtures with face-context images assert `_detector="mediapipe"` and detection.center matches landmark-derived center.
**Sampling:** Every task touching detect.py.
