## Judgment calls (1)

**Strict array-index monotonicity over lenient.** Considered allowing the parser to skip forward (e.g., accept §3 immediately after §2 even without §2.5) as a resilience measure. Rejected because lenient monotonicity defeats the §2.5 instruction in Plan 16 — Sonnet might silently omit §2.5 with no parser pressure to emit it. Strict monotonicity is a forcing function: if Sonnet skips §2.5, EVERYTHING after §2 gets dropped. Founder will see this immediately in UAT-4 if it happens, and we re-instruct. Worth the trade-off; recorded so future regressions don't accidentally relax it.
