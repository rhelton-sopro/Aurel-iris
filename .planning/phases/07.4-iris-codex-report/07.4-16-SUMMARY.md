## Judgment calls (1)

Fixed by replacing `.` with `[\s\S]` and removing the `s` flag — semantically equivalent (matches across newlines), works under any ES target. Recorded as judgment call rather than retroactively bumping tsconfig target — bumping target would touch 200+ files and is out of Plan 16 scope.
