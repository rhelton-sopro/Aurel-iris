## Calibration findings → Phase 7.2 backlog

These are Modal-output-quality concerns, NOT canonicalize mechanism issues. The canonicalization pipeline correctly delivered geometrically consistent inputs to Modal; the items below are about what Modal does WITH those inputs.

### Finding 1 — Digestive halo underweighted in report ordering

**Visual:** Dense yellow-ochre pigmented halo around the pupil (entire reticular zone), bilateral, with brown-medium pigment overlay. Classical biliar constitution expression with hepato-pancreatic-renal load signal.

**Report behavior:** Finding appears diluted across §3 (Indicações Sistêmicas) and §4 (Toxemia) instead of leading §3 and organizing §9 (Cuidados Integrativos).

**Likely root cause:** `vision_features` captures `central_heterochromia` boolean + `dominant_pigments` array, but the LLM system prompt doesn't instruct the model to weight findings by VISUAL PROMINENCE. A pigmented halo with `dominant_pigments=['pigmento_amarelo','pigmento_marrom']` + `heterocromia=true` should trigger a "lead finding" treatment in the constitution and systemic sections.

**Fix scope:** LLM prompt amendment (`apps/web/prompts/system.md`) adding a findings-hierarchy clause — visual prominence drives section ordering, not just feature presence. Coordinate with SPEC.md §6 (D-PR1 frozen contract).

### Finding 2 — Thyroid lacuna metrics overestimated (size)

**Visual:** Real but smaller signals at hour-4 bilateral. Direct measurement vs report's 24-27mm bilateral indicates Modal is over-counting the chromatic transition zone as part of the lacuna extent.

**Report behavior:** Lacuna metrics 24-27mm bilateral.

**Likely root cause:** Modal segment.iris_mask or sectoral detector is including the chromatic transition zone (color gradient at the lacuna boundary) within the lacuna extent. Canonical crop centered the image correctly; the overcount is purely in segmentation logic.

**Fix scope:** Modal `vision-service/pipeline/segment.py` (or wherever lacuna extent is computed) — tighten the inclusion boundary to exclude pixels that fall within a chromatic transition gradient (LAB-delta threshold).

### Finding 3 — Missing findings (no detectors exist)

**Visual:** Two clearly visible signals in source photos absent from Modal's `vision_features`:

**3a. Cramp ring / inner reticular contraction**
- Pattern: Mild contraction ring following the digestive halo (functional digestive tension signal)
- Modal output: not present in any section / features
- **Detector needed:** new module to identify concentric ring artifacts in the inner reticular zone via radial intensity profile + curvature analysis

**3b. Discrete pigment concentrations in lateral ciliary zone (bilateral)**
- Pattern: Localized lymphatic load signal (NOT full tofus — discrete clusters)
- Modal output: `dominant_pigments` captures presence but `sectoral_pigments` is empty
- **Detector needed:** peripheral pigment clustering — sectoral analysis of pigment density at hours 8-10 and 2-4 (ciliary zone)

**Fix scope:** Modal pipeline additions — two new detectors. Both fit naturally inside Phase 7.2 corpus expansion + threshold tuning since they need empirical thresholds derived from ≥3 fixtures per pattern.

## Cross-validation findings (Gemini analysis pass, 2026-05-12)

Founder ran f4408c23 source photos + generated report through Gemini for independent clinical review. Three additional calibration gaps surfaced + one duplicate of the existing findings-hierarchy concern.

### Finding 4 — Laterality detection gap (7.2-E)

**Visual:** Carúncula lacrimal (pink tissue at inner corner of eye, nasal side) is a reliable laterality marker — right eye has carúncula on left side of frame, left eye on right side of frame.

**Pipeline behavior:** Canonicalize Sonnet prompt does not detect carúncula. If a founder uploads an inverted photo (camera rotation 180°, eye labels swapped at capture time, or hardware quirk), every downstream analysis is wrong about sector locations (hour 3 actually means hour 9, etc.) without any error indicator.

**Fix scope (Phase 7.2-E):** Extend `apps/web/lib/canonicalize/sonnet-bbox.ts` SYSTEM_PROMPT to report carúncula position per image. Orchestrator (`apps/web/lib/canonicalize/index.ts`) validates against the user-supplied `reading_images.eye` label; mismatch surfaced via new `canonical_metadata.laterality_mismatch: [{ eye, angle, expected_side, observed_side }]` and admin UI badge. Defensive — does NOT auto-flip; founder reviews and re-captures.

Cross-cutting: this touches canonicalize-side, NOT Modal. Placed in 7.2 per founder routing (broader "pipeline quality" bucket).

### Finding 5 — Nerve tension rings false negative (7.2-F)

**Visual:** Cramp rings visible in the ciliary zone of f4408c23 photos.

**Pipeline behavior:** `vision_features.{eye}.rings.nerve_rings = false`. Modal's ring detector threshold is too conservative.

**Distinct from 7.2-C:** 7.2-C adds a NEW detector for cramp ring in the INNER reticular zone (around the digestive halo). 7.2-F TUNES the EXISTING ring detector in the CILIARY zone. Same family of phenomenon (ring artifacts from neuro-muscular tension), different anatomical location, different detection logic.

**Fix scope (Phase 7.2-F):** Modal ring-detector threshold tuning. Lower activation threshold or add a ciliary-specific branch. Wave C corpus provides empirical thresholds.

### Finding 6 — Collarette shape false negative (7.2-G)

**Visual:** Collarette shows zigue-zague (zigzag) irregularity in f4408c23.

**Pipeline behavior:** `vision_features.{eye}.collarette.shape = 'regular'`. Modal currently has no irregularity classifier — collarette is either present or absent, no shape gradient.

**Fix scope (Phase 7.2-G):** Modal `vision-service/pipeline/` — add collarette irregularity metric (radial-distance variance from circular fit OR curvature inflection count). New output: `collarette.irregularity ∈ {regular, leve_zigue, zigue_zague, severo}`. Empirical thresholds from Wave C corpus.
