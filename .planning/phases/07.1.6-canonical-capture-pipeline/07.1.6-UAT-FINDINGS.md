### Finding 1 — Digestive halo underweighted in report ordering

**Visual:** Dense yellow-ochre pigmented halo around the pupil (entire reticular zone), bilateral, with brown-medium pigment overlay. Classical biliar constitution expression with hepato-pancreatic-renal load signal.

**Report behavior:** Finding appears diluted across §3 (Indicações Sistêmicas) and §4 (Toxemia) instead of leading §3 and organizing §9 (Cuidados Integrativos).

**Likely root cause:** `vision_features` captures `central_heterochromia` boolean + `dominant_pigments` array, but the LLM system prompt doesn't instruct the model to weight findings by VISUAL PROMINENCE. A pigmented halo with `dominant_pigments=['pigmento_amarelo','pigmento_marrom']` + `heterocromia=true` should trigger a "lead finding" treatment in the constitution and systemic sections.

**Fix scope:** LLM prompt amendment (`apps/web/prompts/system.md`) adding a findings-hierarchy clause — visual prominence drives section ordering, not just feature presence. Coordinate with SPEC.md §6 (D-PR1 frozen contract).

### Finding 2 — Thyroid lacuna metrics overestimated (size)

**Visual:** Real but smaller signals at hour-4 bilateral. Direct measurement vs report's 24-27mm bilateral indicates Modal is over-counting the chromatic transition zone as part of the lacuna extent.

**Report behavior:** Lacuna metrics 24-27mm bilateral.

**Likely root cause:** Modal segment.iris_mask or sectoral detector is including the chromatic transition zone (color gradient at the lacuna boundary) within the lacuna extent. Canonical crop centered the image correctly; the overcount is purely in segmentation logic.

**Fix scope:** Modal `vision-service/pipeline/segment.py` (or wherever lacuna extent is computed) — tighten the inclusion boundary to exclude pixels that fall within a chromatic transition gradient (LAB-delta threshold).

### Finding 3 — Missing findings (no detectors exist)

**Fix scope:** Modal pipeline additions — two new detectors. Both fit naturally inside Phase 7.2 corpus expansion + threshold tuning since they need empirical thresholds derived from ≥3 fixtures per pattern.
